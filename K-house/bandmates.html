K-House site materials

This unlisted page collects the practical bits: names, instruments, short bios, photos, recordings, sample repertoire, booking details, and corrections. Everything is optional, and you can submit the form more than once as you think of new material.

Minimum Useful Package

Public identity

Final band name, one-line description, location, and contact person. Current draft: K-House, live jazz from Exeter, contact daaronr@gmail.com.

People

For each person: preferred name, instrument or vocal role, one short 2-4 sentence bio, and any links they are happy to share publicly.

Media

One strong group photo, optional individual photos, and 2-4 short sound or video samples. Informal rehearsal clips are fine for a first pass.

Booking facts

Lineup options, setup needs, travel range, private-event comfort level, jam-session format, and a sensible rate range or sliding-scale policy.

Content Checklist

  • Photos A usable landscape hero image, preferably with the whole group visible and enough empty space for text.
  • Samples 30-90 second clips that show time feel, sound, vocals if used, and the difference between compact and expanded lineups.
  • Repertoire 12-20 representative tunes, not necessarily a full setlist.
  • Events A few plain-language examples: restaurant set, house party, listening room, hosted jam, wedding reception, outdoor gathering.
  • Constraints Any limits we should state clearly: travel radius, power needs, weather cover, earliest setup time, noise/volume expectations.

Send Inputs / Comments

All fields are optional. Submit a quick note now, then come back later with photos, recordings, bios, or corrections. Multiple submissions are fine. You can also comment inline with Hypothes.is or email anything directly to daaronr@gmail.com.
