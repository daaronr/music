12-bar blues companion

Explore blues variations with musical guidance

Compare common 12-bar blues progressions, see where the colors come from, and learn why each variation works. Pick a chorus and the app will label every bar with clear chord families and coaching tips tailored for improvisers and accompanists.

Why this tool?

  • Visual bar-by-bar chord map for instant orientation.
  • Color hints that connect harmony to role (tonic, subdominant, turnaround).
  • Plain-language explanations so players can practice smarter.
